# Inside FedWAM

Open `index.html` to explore the seven-chapter, 4½-minute silent guide. Use chapter buttons, playback, seek and the inspector. Space toggles playback and arrow keys change chapters outside focused controls. Videos have independent controls.

All computations and assets are local. Illustrative computations are labeled as teaching examples; the reference evaluation identifies its model. Recording details are in `assets/evidence.json`.
